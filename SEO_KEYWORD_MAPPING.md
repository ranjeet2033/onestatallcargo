## 1. Master Keyword Strategy Overview

The keyword strategy is built around high-intent commercial and informational logistics queries in India across three primary customer segments:
1. **D2C & E-commerce Brands:** Needing automated courier aggregation, COD remittance, low RTO, and multi-carrier integrations.
2. **B2B Manufacturers & Distributors:** Seeking heavy freight, bulk cargo, LTL/FTL trucking, and corporate supply chain contracts.
3. **Franchise Entrepreneurs:** Seeking profitable courier hub and booking counter franchise business opportunities across Indian districts.

## 4. Internal Linking Architecture Rules

1. **Hierarchy:** Homepage &rarr; Primary Solutions (`/solutions/*`) & Platform (`/platform/*`) &rarr; Detailed Features / Blog Articles &rarr; Conversion Pages (`/register`, `/contact`, `/pricing`, `/franchise`).
2. **Contextual Anchor Texts:** Never use "click here" or "read more". Always use descriptive anchor text matching primary or secondary keywords (e.g., "[B2C courier service in India](/solutions/b2c-shipping)", "[B2B cargo freight](/solutions/b2b-cargo)", "[courier franchise opportunity](/franchise)").
3. **Cross-Linking:**
   - Every solution page links to the API Docs, Pricing Calculator, and Tracking.
   - Every blog article includes at least 3 contextual links to relevant solutions, platform tools, and sign-up/contact CTAs.
   - Platform pages cross-link each other (e.g., NDR links to RTO and COD).
